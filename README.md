# TOE👣

A public record of my exploration into the Big Theory of Everything (Big TOE), consciousness, reality, information, and the nature of existence.

This repository is not intended to present final answers.

It is a collection of questions, observations, hypotheses, connections, and evolving ideas.

The purpose is simple:

Think openly.
Document honestly.
Revise freely.

---

## Why This Repository Exists

Most thoughts disappear.

This repository exists so ideas can be captured, revisited, challenged, expanded, and sometimes discarded.

I am less interested in defending beliefs than improving understanding.

---

## Core Questions

Some recurring questions include:

- What is consciousness?
- What is reality?
- Is information more fundamental than matter?
- What role does observation play in experience?
- How does free will fit into the larger system?
- What is the relationship between mind and physical reality?
- Can a Theory of Everything include subjective experience?

---

## Working Principles

These principles guide the project:

1. Curiosity over certainty.
2. Evidence over preference.
3. Open-mindedness without gullibility.
4. Models are not reality.
5. Every conclusion is provisional.
